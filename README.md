# The Isle of Second Chances — Charm the Cartographers

Entry for the make.fun bounty: https://make.fun/bounty/u-ravendell-charm-the-cartographers-a-one-day-00854547

A hand-rendered parchment treasure map (2048×1536), surveyed in one day and regretted for a year. The entrypoint (`main.py`, standard library only) delivers the committed `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png`.

## What's on the map

**20 labeled places** — Port of Small Hours, Cape of Cold Feet, The Widow's Lighthouse, Mount Meh, Forest of Misplaced Keys, River of Rumors (mind the waterfall), Toll Bridge of Exact Change, Shrine of Saint Maybe, The Debtors' Ruins, Cliff of Pending Approvals, Smugglers' Nap Cove, Village of Borrowed Umbrellas, Lake of Last Sips, Gullwing Market, Caves of Echoing Excuses, Palm Grove of Second Thoughts, Bay of Lost Chargers, The Sunken ATM, Mermaid's Hair Salon, and the Kraken's Reading Room.

**Named creatures, drawn and labeled** — Harold the Sea Monster (humps, wake and all), Mabel the Mermaid on her rock, and the Kraken himself, reading.

**Routes** — the Captain's bold red dashed route runs from the Port of Small Hours through the Village of Borrowed Umbrellas and the Toll Bridge of Exact Change to a large red X: *Captain's Last Laugh — the treasure*. Two blue dashed sailing roads connect the coastal points, with two galleon silhouettes under full sail.

**Furniture** — an 8-point compass rose, a legend box (treasure, routes, villages, forests, and one mostly harmless volcano), a title cartouche, a 100-paces scale bar, rhumb lines, depth soundings, coastal hachures, and a double-rule frame.
